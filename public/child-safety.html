Our Commitment to Child Safety

At Xios, we are fully committed to protecting children from any form of exploitation, abuse, or harmful content. We apply strict safety policies and compliance procedures to ensure a safe digital environment for all users.

Prevention of Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)

  • Any content involving sexual exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or inappropriate communication with minors is forbidden.
  • Sharing suggestive images, videos, or messages involving minors is not allowed.
  • Any violation will result in permanent account termination.

Reporting Harmful Content

Users may report inappropriate content using in-app reporting tools or by emailing our safety team. All reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Handling of CSAM

Any detected CSAM content is removed immediately, accounts are permanently banned, and authorities are notified as required by law.

Privacy & Data Protection for Minors

  • We do not collect minors’ personal data without verified parental consent.
  • All user data is encrypted and securely stored.
  • We comply with international child data protection laws.
